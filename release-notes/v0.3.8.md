## What's Changed

### Modular Architecture, Cycle Cuts & Terminal Safety
- **Terminal & Agent Safe-Output (`src/safe_text.rs`)**: Sanitized untrusted email subjects, senders, bodies, calendar events, tasks, and contacts rendered to CLI terminal output against ANSI/VT100 escape sequences (CSI/OSC/DCS/C1), C0/C1 control characters, Unicode Bidi overrides, and invisible Unicode Tag characters (`U+E0000..=U+E007F`), while preserving raw `--json` output.
- **Supply Chain & Packaging**: Added `[package.metadata.binstall]` for `cargo-binstall`, `deny.toml` + `.github/workflows/audit.yml` (`cargo-deny` CI), and `refs/tags/v` tag guards on release jobs.
- **Codebase Modularization**: Decomposed `src/mcp.rs` (`call_tool` 1,325L → 37L), `src/cli.rs`, `src/ui/app.rs` (`handle_ui_msg` 857L → 55L), and `tests/mock_graph_e2e.rs` into domain submodules and cut 2 `ui/overlays → AppState` cycles (`context_menu`, `mail_compose`).
