## What's Changed

### Performance Optimization Suite (SQLite N+1, TUI Gen-Cache, HTML Cache & Pixel Pipeline)
- **Sync & Storage**: Eliminated N+1 full `get_mail_message` over-fetching in `sync.rs` via lightweight batch existence/body-loaded SQL checks and avoided redundant `LIKE` scans on `>= 3`-char trigram misses.
- **O(1) TUI Caching & HTML Parser**: Replaced per-frame Dual SipHash over full email HTML with `mail_data_gen`/`mail_folders_gen`/`nav_fold_gen` O(1) caches, cached `visible_mail_rows` and `nav_pane` rows, upgraded `HtmlParseCache` to an O(1) intrusive LRU with 8-byte word-chunked hashing, promoted `Selector::parse` to `LazyLock`, deferred `viewport_pixel_payload` in `tui.rs`, and sliced visible viewport rows in preview overlays.
- **Pixel Engine**: Upgraded Sixel quantization to flat 32K histogram + 5-5-5 LUT + 2-row `i16` dithering + fast ASCII RLE, switched PNG encoding to `Fast`/`NoFilter` (`fdeflate`), eliminated per-span `format!` in CLI output, and enabled `opt-level = 3` for codec/pixel crates.
