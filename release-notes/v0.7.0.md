# v0.7.0 — Minimal-Chrome TUI Redesign, Composer-First Ergonomics & Full English Localization

Version `0.7.0` delivers a ground-up **Minimal-Chrome TUI redesign**, a **Composer-first workflow with seamless history scrolling**, **`Ctrl+K` Unified Omni-Switcher**, and **100% English localization** across the TUI, CLI, HTTP API, MCP server, diagnostics, and documentation.

## Minimal-Chrome Visual Architecture & Stream Typography

- **Surface Hierarchy Over Nested Boxes**: Eliminates the redundant top bar, 6-column emoji rail, and double `Borders::ALL` box walls (`││` / `──`) in favor of a **Unified Left Sidebar** (`1 Chats · 2 Teams · 3 Act` + clickable quick-jump/filter bar), **`1`-cell hairlines (`│` / `─`)**, and an **Auto-Sizing Hairline Composer** (`2` rows minimum, expanding dynamically up to `8` body lines) — reclaiming `+4` vertical stream rows and `+7..10` horizontal columns.
- **Cozy 5-Minute Author Grouping & `Ctrl+D` Compact Mode**:
  - **Cozy (default)**: Groups consecutive messages from the same author within 5 minutes under a single header, places channel post subjects (`◆ Subject`) cleanly beneath the author header, omits right code-block borders so terminal mouse drag-copy stays clean, and suppresses noisy thread pills on zero-reply posts.
  - **Compact (`Ctrl+D` instant toggle)**: High-density single-line gutter view (`14:32  Author │ message...`), persisted in `config.toml`.
- **Unread Divider & Non-Destructive Full-Width Selection**: Snapshots `last_read_at` on conversation open to render a `─── N unread messages ───` divider, and tints selected messages edge-to-edge (`bg_selected` + `▎` accent bar + clickable inline `r reply · t thread · + react` hints) without overwriting author, `@me` mention, code, or link colors.
- **Semantic Multi-Depth Themes**: Auto-detects `NO_COLOR`, `Ansi16`, `Ansi256`, and Truecolor (`Midnight Dark` & `Daylight Light`), paired with colorblind-safe presence glyphs (`●` Available, `●` Busy, `⊘` Do Not Disturb, `◐` Away/BRB, `○` Offline).

## Composer-First Workflow & Power-User Navigation

- **Immediate Composer Entry + Free History Scrolling**: Opening any chat or channel focuses the Composer right away while allowing full message history scrolling (`PgUp`/`PgDn`, `Ctrl+Up`/`Ctrl+Down`, `Up`/`Down` when the input buffer is empty, and mouse wheel — including automatic `@odata.nextLink` older-history loading at the top) without leaving the Composer.
- **Direct Tab Switching (`1` / `2` / `3` & `Alt+1..3`)**: Press `1`, `2`, `3` in browse modes (`Nav` / `Chat` / `Thread`) or `Alt+1` / `Alt+2` / `Alt+3` from anywhere (including inside the Composer) to jump straight to **Chats**, **Teams**, or **Activity**.
- **`Ctrl+K` Unified Omni-Switcher**: Fuzzy-jumps across unread/pinned/recent chats, team channels (`eng/gen`), `@` DMs, `>` commands, and `/` message search from one modal.
- **Inbox-Zero Triage & Safe Quit**: Structured sidebar sections (`PINNED` / `UNREAD` / `RECENT` with `p` pin toggle and collapsed team unread rollup badges), `u` / `Alt+u` next-unread jump, `` ` `` last-chat bounce, `Alt+Up/Down` room stepping, `Alt+Left/Right` history navigation, `PasteBurstDetector` for unbracketed multiline pastes, and `Ctrl+Q` safe quit (replacing accidental single-key `q` exits).
- **Bug Fixes**: Removes raw OSC 8 escape sequence corruption inside `ratatui` spans, fixes the Thread pane off-by-one selection mismatch, auto-scrolls the viewport on `j/k` message selection, fixes modal backdrop/button click handling, and fixes `>100%` zoom clamping in image preview.

## 100% English Localization

- Converts all TUI labels/overlays/toasts, `clap` CLI `--help` & command outputs, OAuth & permission diagnostics, error templates, and `README.md` into idiomatic English while preserving full CJK/UTF-8 rendering and SQLite FTS5 search support.
